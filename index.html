03 Early experiment

Fugazi

A tiny language model that borrows the rhythm of a text corpus, one word at a time. order = 2 · seed = 1 Explore project

Why it exists An early step into AI and ML: seeing what a small, understandable text model can generate.

Mechanism
A word-level Markov chain that samples from the words observed after each context in the corpus.
Includes
Seeded repeatable runs, a cache keyed to corpus metadata, and clear errors for unusable input.
Scope
A classical n-gram experiment that runs on Python's standard library, with its limitations documented.
